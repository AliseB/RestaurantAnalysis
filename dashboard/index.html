Which price tier gives a new restaurant the best chance of a high rating?

Zomato puts every restaurant in a price tier from 1 (cheapest) to 4 within its own market, so tiers compare across countries even though costs do not. A high rating is 4.0 or more, Zomato's "Very Good" and "Excellent".

How to read this

  • The data is one snapshot of 9,551 Zomato listings with no dates, so it cannot show trends over time. It shows which restaurants rate well today, not what happens after a restaurant opens.
  • Restaurants with no rating yet (Zomato shows 0) are left out of averages and of the 4.0+ share, but counted in "Not rated".
  • Outside India the dataset holds only 20 to 434 restaurants per country, and they lean toward well-known, highly rated places. Treat those figures as a rough guide.
  • Costs are in each country's own currency, so they are shown only for regions that use one. The source labels Philippine prices "Botswana Pula".
  • All figures come from the SQL in the RestaurantAnalysis repository (sql/03_marts.sql), run in DuckDB.
